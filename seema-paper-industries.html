Our Legacy

Five Decades of Paper & Notebook Expertise.

Founded in 1978, Seema Paper Industries has evolved alongside the notebook manufacturing industry — from conventional manual processes to a modern operation supported by advanced machinery, quality systems and distribution infrastructure.

1978
1978

The beginning

Late Shri R.D. Gupta starts the business in Raigarh, Chhattisgarh, built on a commitment to quality paper products.

GROWTH

From manual to mechanised

The company evolves from manual manufacturing, steadily introducing machinery to raise consistency and output.

QUALITY

ISO 9001:2015 certified

Quality systems formalised across sourcing, production, appraisal and packing.

TODAY

A modern manufacturing operation

A 40,000 sq ft facility, automatic exercise-book and binding equipment, five product brands and depots in Ranchi and Raipur.
